Key Traits

Morphit Flow

Mobility + Stability

“Move freely, control completely.”

Restore range, coordination, and end-range control

Slow tempo, low RPE (5–6), longer eccentrics (3-1-3 / 4-1-2)

Morphit Build

Hypertrophy

“Shape, strengthen, solidify.”

Increase lean muscle and volume tolerance

Moderate RPE (7–8), 8–15 reps, short rest (60–90s)

Morphit Strong

Strength

“Power through precision.”

Maximize force production and neural drive

High RPE (8–9), 4–8 reps, long rest (120–150s)

Morphit Move

Longevity / Balanced

“Train for life, not just today.”

Maintain long-term performance and health

Moderate RPE (7), balanced rep scheme (6–12), mixed tempo
